Unbranded Presentation

Every order is sealed within an unmarked, highly durable exterior parcel. There are no external logos, brand names, or indicators of the contents. The return address remains strictly administrative. The true Velvet & Ember unboxing experience is reserved solely for the interior.

Signature Required

To guarantee possession, all domestic and international shipments require a direct signature upon delivery. We do not permit packages to be left unattended.
